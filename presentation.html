PROBLEM STATEMENT

Friction and Pitfalls in Custom PC Building

Why beginners struggle when assembling their own computer systems

Physical & Electrical Incompatibilities
  • Socket Mismatch: Incompatible CPU and motherboard pin layouts (e.g. Intel LGA1700 vs AMD AM4) result in bent pins and component destruction.
  • RAM Generation Conflicts: DDR4 and DDR5 memory modules feature differing notches, pinouts, and voltages (1.2V vs 1.1V).
  • Chassis Clearance: Full ATX motherboards failing to install inside Micro-ATX cases due to missing standoff heights.
  • Power Supply Deficit: Undersized PSUs leading to transient power tripping and kernel crashes under gaming load.
Financial Tracking Deficiencies
  • Absence of INR Pricing: Existing international tools use USD/EUR, lacking native Indian Rupee (₹) numbering standards.
  • Uncalculated Taxes: Consumers often forget to calculate India's statutory 18% GST, causing surprise budget overruns.
  • No Visual Budgeting: Lack of real-time progress bars or visual cues indicating whether a build is safely within budget.
  • Cluttered Tools: Commercial websites are flooded with affiliate ads, paid paywalls, and slow multi-page reloads.
Core Objective: Provide an automated, deterministic client-side web application that validates hardware compatibility and tracks budgets in real-time.

ALGORITHMIC LOGIC

Core Compatibility & Budget Algorithms

Mathematical constraint modeling and deterministic verification rules

4 Core Compatibility Constraint Checks
Socket Rule \( \text{socket}(\text{CPU}) == \text{socket}(\text{Motherboard}) \)
Flags physical pin mismatches between Intel LGA1700, AMD AM4, and AM5.
RAM Standard \( \text{ramType}(\text{RAM}) \subseteq \text{ramType}(\text{Motherboard}) \)
Guarantees DDR4 RAM is paired with DDR4 boards, and DDR5 with DDR5.
Form Factor \( \text{formFactor}(\text{MB}) \in \text{supported}(\text{Case}) \)
Ensures Full ATX motherboards are not placed into Micro-ATX chassis.
Power Load \( \text{Wattage}_{\text{PSU}} \ge (\text{TDP}_{\text{CPU}} + \text{TDP}_{\text{GPU}} + 100\text{W}) \times 1.30 \)
Calculates continuous draw + 30% headroom for gaming transient spikes.
